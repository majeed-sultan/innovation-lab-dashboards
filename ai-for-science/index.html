Executive perspective

A focused comparison of persistent scientist-facing workbenches—not a ranking of infrastructure providers.

Agentic science workbenches are emerging as a distinct category. The market spans broad enterprise environments, scientist-focused reproducible execution, hypothesis-generation research systems, and domain-oriented R&D companions. The decisive dimensions include evidence grounding, scientific tools and compute execution, reproducibility, connectors, governance and product maturity.

Scope: Microsoft Discovery, Claude Science, OpenAI Rosalind, Google Co-Scientist, Edison Scientific / Kosmos, BIOVIA MARIE, Dotmatics Luma Agent and Bohrium / Bohr Agent.

Assessment criteria and weights

Connector availability is a separate criterion. Generic APIs, SDKs and MCP compatibility are not equivalent to documented vendor-supported ready-to-use connectors.

Market observations

Research memory & scientific assurance

Distinguish source retrieval from persistent, versioned institutional research knowledge. Assess traceability, computational provenance, uncertainty, reproducibility and scientific review.

Agent governance & integration

Compare bounded tool authority, approvals, checkpoints, logs and recovery. Assess packaged connectors separately from configurable adapters and bespoke integrations.

Domain depth vs breadth

Domain-focused workbenches can outperform horizontal approaches within their established scientific data estates, even when their cross-domain breadth is narrower.

Enabling ecosystem (not ranked)

AWS, Databricks, NVIDIA BioNeMo/PhysicsNeMo, Rescale and laboratory automation providers may enable scientific workflows but are not ranked as standalone agentic science workbenches here.
