Methodology

This is a technology-dependency and market-navigation map, not a league table, readiness ranking or investment screen. Every record has one primary layer and may participate in several secondary layers.

Primary entriesRecords whose primaryLayer equals the selected layer. Directory sections and the detailed PowerPoint use this count.
Participating recordsRecords whose primary or secondary layers include the selected layer. Choose this broader capability count with the layer-relationship filter.
  • The 80 baseline records were retained for source continuity, but names, status, evidence and classifications were audited against current sources.
  • 103 additional records cover foundries, controls, cryogenics, networks, post-quantum security, sensing and enterprise integration.
  • Official sites are linked for 182 records. One legacy record is retained without a current quantum-company site and is clearly marked.
  • Ownership coverage is deliberately selective: it captures disclosed acquisitions and operating brands relevant to capability mapping, not every shareholder or corporate relationship.

Coverage boundaries

Records include private and public pure plays, incumbents, business units, suppliers, services firms, network operators, acquired teams and operating brands. Use organization-type and market-status filters to compare like with like.
